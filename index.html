Your room is the mnemonic.

The method of loci works because spatial memory outlasts everything else. LOCI puts your notes on the walls you already know — and a note's appearance is its memory strength. As you begin to forget, it fades. Your room becomes a live readout of what your brain is about to lose.

  1. Author a note here. You never type in a headset.
  2. Place it: look at a wall, a table, a shelf — pinch where it lives.
  3. Recall by gazing at a fading note and pinching to bring it back.
